# Daytime background artwork

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. Original fighter art remains unchanged.

Shared brief: Detailed wide panoramic side-view anime fighting-game background. Sunny daytime, blue/pink/lavender palette, delicate clean lines and painted environmental textures. No characters, interfaces, writing, logos, or rooftops. Keep a continuous level stone path across the bottom 18 percent, with scenery behind the path.

- **Sakura Park:** A magnificent pink cherry-blossom tree, spring neighborhood park, blue sky and white clouds, lavender hills, canal, wooden fence, bench, flowers, and tiled Japanese houses.
- **Harajuku Lane:** Sunny modern Japanese neighborhood, pastel shops and cafes, striped awnings, balconies, distant utility wires, flowering trees, flower pots, parked bicycle, and overhead pennants.
- **Lavender Garden:** Japanese garden, lavender-roofed pavilion, distant torii, cherry blossoms, stone lantern, wisteria, maple greenery, pond, mountains, and clear blue afternoon sky.

Game-ready PNG files are in `public/assets/stages/`. The renderer adds drifting petals, moving wisps, soft sunlight and pond glints without changing the artwork files.

## Neon Tokyo revision

Built-in image generation: three wide side-view anime Tokyo backgrounds at night. Cobalt, midnight blue and muted teal, cyan city lights, restrained lavender/magenta, darker quiet central areas so pale skin and pink/purple fighters stand out. No interface, logos, readable writing or fighters. Broad open foreground paths.

- Neon Arcade: Akihabara-inspired shops, illuminated signs, upper balconies, wires, blue-lit sakura.
- Canal District: city reflections, elevated train bridge, traditional Japanese buildings and blue glass towers.
- Skyline Plaza: Shibuya-inspired skyscrapers, distant Tokyo Tower, modern structures and violet sakura.

New files: `neon-arcade.png`, `neon-canal.png`, `neon-skyline.png`. Original daytime assets remain available for comparison.

## Mall and motorcycle street

Built-in image generation with the same cool-blue side-view Tokyo palette and clear foreground lane:

- **Neon Mall:** Indoor anime atrium, cyan architectural lighting, ice cream shop and bubble tea cafe, glass mezzanine, small calm adult shoppers, drinks and seated patrons in the background.
- **Cat & Moto Street:** Japanese motorcycle shop, parked sport bikes and helmets, small distant pedestrians, three sitting cats (calico, orange tabby, black-and-white), and a collectible-style Satoru Gojo statue from Jujutsu Kaisen with white spiky hair, blindfold and high-collared outfit on a pedestal.

Game assets: `anime-mall.png`, `tokyo-cat-street.png`, and `mall-walker-0.png` through `mall-walker-3.png` in `public/assets/stages/`. Prior background files remain for comparison.
